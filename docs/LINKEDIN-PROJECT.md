Warraq | Arabic PDF Q&A & Retrieval Assistant

Arabic-first PDF app with browser extraction, page-level sources, keyword-based retrieval, and server-side Gemini integration. Prepared for GitHub-to-Vercel deployment. Includes an extractive demo and 23 automated tests. A live Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite request was verified with a synthetic document. OCR and embeddings are planned extensions.
